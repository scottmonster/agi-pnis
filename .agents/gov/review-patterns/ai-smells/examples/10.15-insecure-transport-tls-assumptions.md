# Insecure transport/TLS assumptions

## 1. Catalog record

- **Catalog identifier:** 10.15
- **Evidence:** e
- **Definition:** Sends sensitive traffic without proper encryption or accepts invalid certificates, protocols, or hostname checks.

## 2. Scenario

### 2.1 Context

A backend calls a partner service with customer details.

### 2.2 Safer approach

Use HTTPS with normal certificate and hostname verification, current protocol settings, and a deliberate trust configuration where a private CA is required.

### 2.3 Failure mode

The agent disables certificate verification to work around a local development certificate error and leaves that setting enabled.

### 2.4 Why this difference matters

Attackers can intercept or impersonate the partner service, exposing or altering sensitive traffic.

## 3. Boundaries and distinctions

Local test environments may need a controlled trust setup, but bypassing certificate or hostname validation is not an equivalent production solution.
