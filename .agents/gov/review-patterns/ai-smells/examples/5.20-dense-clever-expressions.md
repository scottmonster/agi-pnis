# Dense “clever” expressions

## 1. Catalog record

- **Catalog identifier:** 5.20
- **Evidence:** e
- **Definition:** Nested comprehensions/chains/lambdas trade readability for compactness.

## 2. Scenario

### 2.1 Context

A billing calculation combines filtering, grouping, fallback selection, currency conversion, and rounding in one nested expression.

### 2.2 Safer approach

Use well-named intermediate values or a focused helper so each business step is inspectable and testable.

### 2.3 Failure mode

Append another nested conditional and lambda to preserve a one-line calculation.

### 2.4 Why this difference matters

Reviewers cannot readily verify precedence, edge cases, or the intended business rule.

## 3. Boundaries and distinctions

A concise expression is fine when it remains clear to local readers. This failure is compactness that obscures behavior and reasoning.
