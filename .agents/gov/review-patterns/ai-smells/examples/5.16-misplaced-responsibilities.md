# Misplaced responsibilities

## 1. Catalog record

- **Catalog identifier:** 5.16
- **Evidence:** se
- **Definition:** Behavior lives far from the data/domain concept it governs.

## 2. Scenario

### 2.1 Context

An HTTP route manually determines whether an order can be cancelled by inspecting status, shipment, and refund fields.

### 2.2 Safer approach

Place the cancellation eligibility behavior with the order domain policy and let the route invoke it.

### 2.3 Failure mode

Copy the field checks into a second route for bulk cancellation.

### 2.4 Why this difference matters

Rules drift from their governing concept and different entry points reach different decisions.

## 3. Boundaries and distinctions

Adapters may translate domain behavior for their layer. Responsibility is misplaced when a rule is owned away from the data or concept it governs without a boundary reason.
