# Too many branches

## 1. Catalog record

- **Catalog identifier:** 5.3
- **Evidence:** e
- **Definition:** Large switch/if trees accumulate instead of clearer responsibility boundaries.

## 2. Scenario

### 2.1 Context

One notification function has a growing conditional tree for email, SMS, push, digest, retry, locale, and account-state cases.

### 2.2 Safer approach

Group stable, distinct delivery responsibilities behind focused dispatch paths and keep shared policy explicit.

### 2.3 Failure mode

Append more conditions to the same tree for every new channel and exception.

### 2.4 Why this difference matters

The branch combinations become hard to reason about and leave paths untested.

## 3. Boundaries and distinctions

A small closed conditional is often clearest. This concerns a large accumulating tree that conceals distinct responsibilities.
