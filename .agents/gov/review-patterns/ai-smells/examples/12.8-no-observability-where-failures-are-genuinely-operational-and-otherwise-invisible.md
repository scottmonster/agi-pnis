# No observability where failures are genuinely operational and otherwise invisible

## 1. Catalog record

- **Catalog identifier:** 12.8
- **Evidence:** se
- **Definition:** Omits the logs, metrics, traces, or health signals needed to detect and diagnose production failures.

## 2. Scenario

### 2.1 Context

A background invoice export runs asynchronously and silently drops failures after its retry limit, with no metric, error event, or health signal for operators.

### 2.2 Safer approach

Expose a failure metric and actionable error event for exhausted exports, then connect the signal to the team’s normal operational monitoring.

### 2.3 Failure mode

Rely on the absence of user reports to conclude that the background export is healthy.

### 2.4 Why this difference matters

Failed exports can remain undetected until customers discover missing invoices, when diagnosis has become slower and recovery more costly.

## 3. Boundaries and distinctions

Not every internal step needs telemetry. This applies when failure has operational impact and cannot otherwise be detected or diagnosed.
