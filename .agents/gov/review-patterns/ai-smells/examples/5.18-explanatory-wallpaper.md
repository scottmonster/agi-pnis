## 2. Scenario

### 2.2 Safer approach

Remove syntax narration and document the non-obvious retention requirement where the deletion occurs.

### 2.4 Why this difference matters

## 3. Boundaries and distinctions

Comments are valuable for invariants, constraints, and decisions not evident from code. The failure is commentary that only restates obvious syntax.
