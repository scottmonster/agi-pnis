# Episode Standard

## 1. Purpose

This standard defines the shared requirements for material episode records.

## 2. Definitions

### 2.1 Material episode and materiality

A material episode is an action, omission, condition, judgement, change, or detected failure that materially affects or could materially affect an applicable requirement, constraint, expected behavior, governing direction, outcome, or prevention decision.

Information is material when it is needed to understand, establish, qualify, correct, or respond to that episode.

### 2.2 Violation

A Violation is an action, omission, condition, or materially incorrect assumption that departs from an applicable requirement, constraint, or expected behavior and warrants correction or other response.

A Violation may be established before its Corrective Action is known. A completed Violation record includes or inherits at least one recommended Corrective Action established through its Episode Investigation.

### 2.3 Concern

A Concern is an action, omission, condition, or material change that does not constitute a Violation but departs from, alters, or calls into question an expected state or prior judgement sufficiently to warrant attention, review, or other proportionate response.

### 2.4 Corrective Action

A Corrective Action is an action recommended or taken in response to a Violation to correct the departure, repair or limit its effects, restore the required state, or reduce the likelihood of recurrence.

### 2.5 Episode Investigation

An Episode Investigation is a bounded, evidence-based inquiry into a detected episode that establishes the expected and observed behavior, relevant context and impact, available explanations, and prevention loci.

An Episode Investigation is required for every Violation and Concern.

## 3. Shared requirements

### 3.1 Evidence and limitations

Records `MUST` distinguish evidence, inference, hypothesis, and unknown information.

Records `MUST` include only materially applicable information.

When material information is unavailable, unknown, not collected, unsafe to retain, or not applicable, the record `MUST` state that limitation rather than inventing a value.

### 3.2 Safe retention

Sensitive or voluminous evidence `MUST` be retained as a safe concise summary or reference when the full material cannot safely or appropriately be included.

### 3.3 Relationships and lineage

Records `MUST` link material related records, work, and artifacts when those relationships are needed to understand the episode.

An update record `MUST` identify its immediately preceding record and preserve the required lineage for its record family.

### 3.4 Immutability

Records are immutable.

A later material change `MUST` create a linked update record rather than alter or delete the preceding record.

### 3.5 Record-family rules

Failures are always material Violations.

The applicable record-family standard determines the status of a judgement record or judgement update record.
