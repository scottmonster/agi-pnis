# Broad renaming churn

## 1. Catalog record

- **Catalog identifier:** 11.4
- **Evidence:** se
- **Definition:** Renames unrelated identifiers or concepts in a focused change, increasing review and merge burden.

## 2. Scenario

### 2.1 Context

A request adds a required `country` field to customer registration.

### 2.2 Safer approach

The agent updates the registration model, handler, and tests, retaining unrelated established names.

### 2.3 Failure mode

The agent also renames `customer` to `client` throughout unrelated billing and reporting modules.

### 2.4 Why this difference matters

The rename obscures the requested work, creates avoidable conflicts, and increases the chance of missed references.

## 3. Boundaries and distinctions

A rename required by the new contract is in scope; this failure concerns unrelated terminology changes bundled into a focused task.
