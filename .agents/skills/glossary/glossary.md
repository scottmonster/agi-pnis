# Glossary

- Failure Record (FR) - A record of a detected failure.
- Failure Update Record (FUR) - A record of later material information that changes, corrects, supplements, or identifies an error in the immediately preceding Failure Record or Failure Update Record.
- Judgement Record (JR) - A record that preserves a material, non-obvious exercise of discretion that governs future work within an explicit scope.
- Judgement Update Record (JUR) - A record of later material information that changes, corrects, supplements, or identifies an error in the immediately preceding Judgement Record or Judgement Update Record.
