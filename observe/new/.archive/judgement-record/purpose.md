# Purpose

## Core purpose

Enable agents to preserve reliable evidence of completed material judgments and their later material outcomes, so people can analyze judgment quality and improve agent behavior over time.

## Original intent and enduring need

Agents can make consequential judgments under different instructions, tools, constraints, and repository conditions. Without a durable record of the completed judgment, its basis, and its later outcome, people cannot reliably distinguish isolated errors from recurring judgment problems or determine what caused them.

## Beneficiaries and use context

People responsible for agent behavior, reliability, safety, and usefulness use this capability when agents complete work that involved a material judgment or when later work materially affects that judgment.

## Intended durable outcome

A future reviewer can determine what judgment was made, why it was justified at the time, what conditions shaped it, what later happened, and whether the outcome met the relevant need without rewriting historical evidence.

## Essential identity commitments

- Preserve only completed material judgments and material later events, so active discussion and routine work do not become misleading historical records.
- Preserve published judgment and follow-up records without later edits, so analysis can distinguish contemporaneous judgment from later knowledge.
- Preserve the difference between evidence, inference, hypothesis, uncertainty, authority, and contribution, so records do not make unsupported causal or human-authorship claims.

## Material scope and boundaries

This skill applies to Material Judgment Records and Judgment Follow-up Records governed by the project's judgment-record policy. It captures material judgment episodes and their outcomes, not general execution telemetry.

## Not the purpose

- Decide material questions for an authorized person or convert agent proposals into user judgments.
- Record every agent action, internal choice, or routine implementation event.

## Purpose basis

- Created from the repository owner's request to collect reliable evidence about agent judgments, their conditions, and their outcomes for later analysis and improvement.
