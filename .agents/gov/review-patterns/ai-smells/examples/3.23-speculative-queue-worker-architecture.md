# Speculative queue/worker architecture

## 1. Catalog record

- **Catalog identifier:** 3.23
- **Evidence:** se
- **Definition:** Adds queues and background workers to work that is cheap and synchronous.

## 2. Scenario

### 2.1 Context

Saving a profile also sends a small internal notification in milliseconds.

### 2.2 Safer approach

Perform the notification in the request flow with suitable error handling.

### 2.3 Failure mode

Deploy a broker, job schema, worker, retry policy, and monitoring for the cheap notification.

### 2.4 Why this difference matters

The queue introduces delivery, retry, and operational failure modes that exceed the work being deferred.

## 3. Boundaries and distinctions

Workers are useful for expensive, unreliable, or decoupled work; cheap synchronous work lacks that operational justification.
