# Low Compression

Use the following low compression guidance while applying any other rules or requirements.
Treat reduced size as a possible result, but not the primary objective.
We do want a smaller artifact but not at the cost of removing material content.
Produce the clearest, least complex form that fully satisfies that boundary.

## Secondary Objective

A secondary objective is not the main goal but rather something that should be attempted while still proritizing the operational boundry and constraints.

- **Use no more tokens than necessary:** Prefer the shortest expressions that satisfy scope requirements.
