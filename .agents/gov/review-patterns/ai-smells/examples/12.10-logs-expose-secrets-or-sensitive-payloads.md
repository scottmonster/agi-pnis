# Logs expose secrets or sensitive payloads

## 1. Catalog record

- **Catalog identifier:** 12.10
- **Evidence:** se
- **Definition:** Records credentials or personal/confidential data in operational telemetry without a justified protected path.

## 2. Scenario

### 2.1 Context

An authentication middleware logs the full request headers after a failed login, including the bearer token and a customer email address.

### 2.2 Safer approach

Redact authorization headers and sensitive fields, logging only the identifiers and error context needed for the incident under the system’s approved protections.

### 2.3 Failure mode

Log the entire failed request to make debugging faster without considering who can access or retain the telemetry.

### 2.4 Why this difference matters

Credentials and confidential data spread to log stores, dashboards, and support workflows, increasing exposure and cleanup obligations.

## 3. Boundaries and distinctions

Useful security or audit telemetry may be retained through a justified protected path. The failure is exposing sensitive content in ordinary operational logs without that justification.
