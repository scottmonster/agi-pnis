# Purpose

This skill records material episodes involving judgement or failure. It replaces the earlier overgrown episode design with one focused entry point that routes work to the applicable judgement or failure rules.

Users, agents, reviewers, and later improvement work benefit from durable records of material judgements, their updates, detected failures, and failure updates. The skill creates immutable JR, JUR, FR, and FUR records so later work can understand what changed and why.

The skill is designed to work across applicable runtimes without relying on provider telemetry, a particular tool stack, or a specific storage product. It does not treat routine work, tool logs, private reasoning, or generated records as policy. It records only material, safely retainable information and preserves meaningful uncertainty.
