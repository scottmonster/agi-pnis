# Brittle snapshot/golden tests

## 1. Catalog record

- **Catalog identifier:** 9.7
- **Evidence:** se
- **Definition:** Large snapshots are updated mechanically rather than inspected semantically.

## 2. Scenario

### 2.1 Context

A report renderer emits an invoice with prices, taxes, and customer details.

### 2.2 Safer approach

Assert important labels and amounts, and review a small intentional golden-file change for layout-sensitive output.

### 2.3 Failure mode

Regenerate a thousand-line snapshot after every failure and commit it without checking what changed.

### 2.4 Why this difference matters

Incorrect totals and missing sections can be accepted as ordinary snapshot churn.

## 3. Boundaries and distinctions

Snapshots are valuable for stable, reviewable representations, but their size and update discipline must preserve semantic review.
